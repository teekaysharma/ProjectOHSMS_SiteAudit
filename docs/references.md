# Reference documents

Primary sources the tool's rules must be traced to. Licensed texts (ISO standards, codes
of practice) are read in session and are not committed to this repo; what the tool
implements from them is recorded in the spec with a clause citation.

| # | Document | Why the tool needs it | Informs | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | The owner's audit methodology: definitions of scores 0 to 5, how findings are graded, the basis for the §5.4 bands, what triggers stop-work | Score meaning and NC handling must follow a written method, not code defaults | C7, C8, §5.4 | Requested |
| R2 | ISO 19011:2018, Guidelines for auditing management systems | Audit evidence, findings, conclusions, sampling and coverage | C8, C11, reports | Requested |
| R3 | ISO 45001:2018 | Internal audit and nonconformity/corrective action requirements the management audit checks against | C11, management catalogue | Requested |
| R4 | Current ADOSH codes of practice and the OSH system framework for the salvaged site sections (permits, temporary works, excavation, plant and lifting, traffic, signage, HV and electrical, testing and commissioning, heat stress, competency, PPE, emergency response) | Checking the technical limits written into the questions | C9 | Requested |
| R5 | VisualRiskAssessor report disclaimer | The two tools' reports are presented together and must not contradict | C3 | Requested |
