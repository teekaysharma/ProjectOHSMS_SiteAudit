Cabinet Resolution No. (3) of 2016
Regarding the UAE Scheme for the Control of Personal Protective
Equipment, Tools, and Materials and Occupational Safety and Health

The Cabinet:
− Having reviewed the Constitution;
− Federal Law No. (1) of 1972 Regarding the Competences of Ministries and Powers of
Ministers, as amended;
− Federal Law No. (28) of 2001 Regarding the Establishment of the Emirates Authority for
Standardization and Metrology, as amended;
− Federal Law No. (24) of 2006 Regarding Consumer Protection, as amended;
− Cabinet Resolution No. (35) of 2015 Regarding the UAE Regulation of Control on
Conformity Assessment Bodies; and
− Upon the approval of the Cabinet,
Hereby resolves as follows:

Article (1)
Definitions
For the purpose of implementing the provisions of this Resolution, the following terms and
expressions shall have the meanings assigned to each of them, unless the context requires
otherwise:
State : The United Arab Emirates.
Authority : The Emirates Authority for Standardization and Metrology.
Board : The Board of Directors of the Authority.
Competent
Authority
: The federal or local authority concerned with implementing the
provisions of this Resolution.

Product : Any equipment, tool, or material worn, carried, or used in any
manner for personal protection, to enhance occupational safety
and health, or to prevent risks to personal safety or health,
whether circulated independently or in conjunction with
another product not used for personal protection.
Standard
Specification
: A document specifying the attributes, descriptions,
characteristics, quality level, dimensions and measurements, or
safety and security requirements of a good, material, service, or
anything subject to measurement. It also includes terminology,
symbols, te st methods, sampling, packaging, labelling, and
marks.
Approved
Standard
Specifications
: The Standard Specifications adopted by the Authority and
referred to as United Arab Emirates Standard Specifications, and
designated by the symbol (UAE.S) or (م  ق/ أ ع م.)
Emirates
Conformity
Assessment
Scheme (ECAS)
: The scheme issued by the Board for the purpose of verifying
whether the Product satisfies the requirements specified in the
Approved Standard Specifications, directly or indirectly, through
specific procedures conducted by the Authority, such as
examination, testing, calibration, or the issuance of Certificates
of Conformity.
Certificate of
Conformity
: A certificate issued by the Authority confirming that a Product,
or any batch thereof, conforms to the requirements of the
Approved Standard Specification.
Emirates Quality
Mark
: The mark approved by the Authority and granted to a Product to
indicate its conformity with the Approved Standard
Specifications applicable to its field.
Supplier : A manufacturer, supplier, or any person whose activity affects
the characteristics of the Product, including the commercial or
legal representative of any of them, as applicable.

Supply Chain : All stages through which the Product passes, from initial
production through to its delivery to the consumer, including its
manufacture, preparation, processing, filling, packaging,
equipping, transportation, storage, distribution, provision,
display, and sale. It also includes the stages of its import and
export, and any other stage related to the Product reaching the
consumer.
Placing on the
Market
: Any activity intended to sell, lease, circulate, store, display,
market, promote, or supply the Product to the consumer,
whether for consideration or free of charge.

Article (2)
Scope of Application
1. The provisions of this Resolution shall apply to the Product throughout all stages of the
Supply Chain in order to ensure the protection of the health and safety of its users.
2. The following Products shall be excluded from the scope of application of this Resolution:
a. Products intended for use by members of the Armed Forces, security forces, or civil
defense forces, or for use at nuclear power plants;
b. Products intended for personal protection against abnormal weather conditions; and
c. Products intended for use aboard marine vessels or gliders.

Article (3)
Product Classification
The risk level of the Product shall be classified in accordance with the Approved Standard
Specifications set forth in the Annex attached to this Resolution.

Article (4)
Responsibilities of the Supplier
At every stage of the Supply Chain, the Supplier shall comply with the following:
=== PAGE 4 ===


Cabinet Resolution of 2016 Regarding the UAE Scheme for the Control of Personal Protective Equipment,

4

1. Carry on its activity through a registered company or sole establishment holding a license
issued in accordance with the legislation in force in the State;
2. Ensure that the instruction manual and operating manual accompany the Product during
the stage for which the Supplier is responsible, in accordance with Article (5) of this
Resolution;
3. Take the necessary measures to ensure that the Product conforms to the requirements of
the Approved Standard Specification set forth in the Annex attached to this Resolution;
and
4. Cooperate with the employees of the Authority and the Competent Authorities and
provide the required documents, including test reports, where necessary.

Article (5)
Instructions and Operating Manual
1. Instructions and operating or user manuals, as applicable, shall accompany the Product in
accordance with the Approved Standard Specifications set forth in the Annex attached to
this Resolution.
2. The instruction and operating manual shall satisfy the following conditions:
a. Meet the general safety requirements for use and any necessary additional
maintenance requirements;
b. Be written in both Arabic and English;
c. Be legible and not readily removable where affixed to the Product; and
d. Have content that does not conflict with the traditions or social and religious customs
prevailing in the State.

Article (6)
Conformity Assessment Requirements
1. The Supplier shall, as applicable, register all Products subject to this Resolution in
accordance with the Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme (ECAS).
2. For the Product to obtain a Certificate of Conformity, the Supplier shall comply with the
=== PAGE 5 ===


Cabinet Resolution of 2016 Regarding the UAE Scheme for the Control of Personal Protective Equipment,
Tools, and Materials and Occupational Safety and Health
5

following:
a. Use the forms approved by the Authority;
b. Satisfy all the requirements of this Resolution and the Approved Standard
Specifications set forth in the Annex attached hereto;
c. Take appropriate measures to establish systems for controlling the Product
throughout the various stages of production, in a manner that ensures continuous
compliance with the requirements of the Approved Standard Specifications; and
d. Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraphs (1) and (2) of this Article, a Product
bearing the Emirates Quality Mark or any other mark recognized by the Authority shall
be deemed to satisfy the requirements of the Approved Standard Specifications.

Article (7)
Control and Market Surveillance
1. The Authority and the Competent Authorities may take samples of any Product Placed on
the Market to verify its conformity with the requirements of this Resolution.
2. Where a Product Placed on the Market does not conform to the requirements of this
Resolution, the Authority or the Competent Authority may take appropriate measures in
respect of the non -conforming Product. Such measures shall include withdrawing the
Product from the market and revoking its Certificate of Conformity. The Supplier shall bear
all costs arising therefrom.
3. Where the source of a non-conforming Product cannot be identified, the seller from whom
the samples were taken shall be responsible for the Product’s non -conformity with the
requirements of this Resolution, unless the seller proves otherwise within the per iod
determined by the Authority or the Competent Authority.

Article (8)
Penalties and Sanctions
Where any provision or requirement of this Resolution is violated, the Authority or the
Competent Authority, as applicable, shall take appropriate measures to remedy the effects of
=== PAGE 6 ===


Cabinet Resolution of 2016 Regarding the UAE Scheme for the Control of Personal Protective Equipment,

6

the violation. Such measures shall include the following:
1. Requiring the person responsible for the violation to withdraw the Product from the
market to rectify the violation, requiring the Product to be returned to its country of origin
if imported, or destroying it if locally manufactured, in accordance with the  procedures
and within the period determined by the Authority for this purpose;
2. Following up on the implementation of all measures required to withdraw the Product or
any other measures required by the Authority in accordance with Clause (1) of this Article.
The person who committed the violation shall bear all fees and costs arising therefrom, as
determined by the Authority; and
3. Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by the legislation in force, any
person who violates the provisions of this Resolution shall be subject to the penalties
prescribed in Federal Law No. (28) of 2001 Regarding the Establishment of the E mirates
Authority for Standardization and Metrology, as amended.

Article (9)
Transitional Provisions
1. The Supplier shall, as applicable, register with the Authority any Product that had entered
any stage of the Supply Chain before the issuance of this Resolution, in accordance with
the Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme (ECAS), within a period not excee ding one
hundred and eighty (180) days from the date of publication of this Resolution in the
Official Gazette.
2. A product placed on the market prior to the issuance of this Resolution shall be granted a
period not exceeding one year from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette for
its disposal, in accordance with the requirements determined by the Author ity, where its
status cannot be regularized in accordance with the provisions of this Resolution.

Article (10)
General Provisions
1. For the purpose of implementing the provisions of this Resolution, the Standard
=== PAGE 7 ===


Cabinet Resolution of 2016 Regarding the UAE Scheme for the Control of Personal Protective Equipment,

7

Specifications set forth in the Annex attached hereto shall be mandatory in the State. The
Board may amend such Standard Specifications or adopt any other Standard Specification
required for implementing this Resolution.
2. The Authority shall develop the procedures and determine the requirements for the
application of conformity-assessment models according to the risk level of the Product.
3. The Competent Authorities shall monitor the Placing of the Product on the Market.
4. The Authority shall be responsible for receiving, examining and approving applications for
Product registration and conformity. The Authority may delegate any Competent
Authority or appoint an Accepted Conformity Assessment Body to assess the conformity
of the Product, in accordance with the legislation adopted in this regard.
5. The Competent Authority in each Emirate shall undertake the surveillance measures
necessary to implement the requirements of this Scheme.
6. This Resolution shall not preclude inspectors of the Competent Authorities from
conducting surveillance or taking samples for other examinations in implementation of
other legislation.
7. All entities subject to this Resolution shall provide inspectors of the Competent Authorities
with all assistance and information they request in connection with the implementation
of its provisions.
8. The Director General of the Authority shall issue such decision as they deem appropriate
where any matter arises that cannot be addressed under the provisions of this Resolution
or any dispute arises regarding its interpretation or application.
9. No Product may be Placed on the Market unless it has obtained a Certificate of Conformity
in accordance with the Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme (ECAS).

Final Provisions
Article (11)
Any provision that contradicts or conflicts with the provisions of this Resolution is hereby
repealed.
